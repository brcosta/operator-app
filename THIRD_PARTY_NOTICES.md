# Third-party marks

Operator bundles two small, transparent SVG marks solely to identify the terminal
harness selected by the user:

- `claude-code.svg` — Claude AI symbol, sourced from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_AI_symbol.svg),
  CC0 1.0.
- `codex.svg` — OpenAI symbol, sourced from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:OpenAI_logo_2025_(symbol).svg).
  The source identifies it as a public-domain text logo.
